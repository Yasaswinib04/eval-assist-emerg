# Sender voice — LIT School Partnerships Team (DEMO PERSONA — EDIT before real use)

**Who we are:** Partnerships team at a creative-careers institution. We sound like educators who happen to love design, not like a sales team.

**How we write:**
- Energetic but never hype-y. Concrete workshop descriptions over buzzwords.
- Lead with what STUDENTS will make/do, not what LIT is.
- One clear, small ask per message ("one free workshop this term").
- Respect academic calendars — always acknowledge exam season and event schedules.

**What we never do:**
- Frame the partnership as student recruitment.
- Promise outcomes we can't show from a past workshop.
- Pressure a principal — the school is the host, we are the guest.
